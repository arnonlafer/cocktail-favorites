SCOFFLAW 
1.5 oz. (45 ml) Rye Whiskey 
1 oz. (30 ml) Dry Vermouth 
1/2 oz. (15 ml) lemon juice 
1/4 oz. (7.5 ml) grenadine 
2 dashes of Orange Bitters 
Orange zest for garnish 
 
 
1. Begin with a shaking tin. 
2. Add 1.5 oz. of rye whiskey. 
3. Add 1 oz. of dry vermouth. 
4. Next, add 1/2 oz. of lemon juice. 
5. Then add 1/4 oz. of grenadine. 
6. Pour in two dashes of orange bitters. 
7. Add ice and shake. 
8. Double strain into your glass. 
9. Peel a swath of orange over the glass so the oils are expressed over the cocktail. 
10. Cut the edges of the orange swath and express over the top of the glass. 
11. Fold the swath onto a cocktail skewer and place it on the glass rim.
